Not all storm damage is tornado damage.

A storm can leave behind tornado damage, straight-line wind damage, hail, and flooding, sometimes in the same neighborhood. Telling them apart IS important. A tornado leaves a narrow, specific path, and finding that path shows where the worst and most dangerous damage is.

Our drone flies the area, maps the damage, and uses AI to trace the tornado’s path: which damage belongs to it and which doesn’t. That means fewer people walking into unstable ground just to find out.

We’re designing it around what responders actually need, not just around what’s fun to build.

How it works

Fly

A Pixhawk flight controller keeps the drone steady while a pilot flies it over the damage.

See

Three cameras capture the scene, including one high-detail camera for data collection.

Send

A Raspberry Pi onboard streams video and flight data over Wi‑Fi to a ground station.

Classify

AI trained on disaster imagery labels structural damage, debris, downed trees, and blocked roads.

Trace

Damage is mapped across the whole area to find the tornado’s path and separate it from other storm damage.
